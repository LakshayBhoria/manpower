A recruitment desk that knows the work

Founded in 2014 in Gurugram, Apex Workforce Solutions began with a team of 20 electricians for a single contractor. Today we recruit, test, train and mobilise thousands of workers every year.

Our mission is simple: give employers dependable people, and give workers fair, safe and documented jobs.

  • Registered recruitment agency, licence no. RA-2014-0457 (sample)
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified processes (sample)
  • In-house trade testing and safety induction
  • Payroll, insurance and accommodation support
  • Dedicated account manager for every client
